# Local competition datasets (never committed)

Download your own official competition data from:
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/MDA-Ethical-AI-KAUST-Academy/data

Place the authorized `train.csv`, `test.csv`, and `sample_submission.csv` under `data/raw/`.
Place your private archived V13 reference CSV under `data/private/v13_reference.csv`.

These folders are intentionally excluded by `.gitignore`. Do not redistribute source CCTV/count data without permission.
